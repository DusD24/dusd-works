Cluckworks

Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Cluckworks is a single-player farm-building game made by DusD Works for players of all ages. This policy covers the apps for iPhone, iPad, and Fire tablets, and the browser and desktop editions.

Gameplay

Cluckworks has no Cluckworks account, advertising, analytics, or chat. Gameplay in the installed apps works offline. App store features and Game Center may need a connection.

Saved games

The game saves your farm and settings in the app or browser on your device. Your device may include app data in its backups according to your settings.

A farm code is a text copy of your game progress. You can export it and choose where to paste or share it. It holds only game data, such as buildings and coins.

Optional Full Farm purchase

Cluckworks offers one optional purchase that opens six more plots for expansion. The Apple App Store handles purchases on iPhone and iPad, and the Amazon Appstore handles purchases on Fire tablets. These stores handle payment and restoring purchases under their own privacy policies.

Cluckworks checks purchase status and saves ownership and pricing information on the device. On Fire tablets, the app also saves the Amazon account identifier returned by the store, so ownership stays with the right account.

Optional Game Center on iPhone and iPad

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Cluckworks reports earned achievements to Game Center and keeps a record on the device of which achievements it has reported for your Game Center player. You can play the game without Game Center.

Children

Cluckworks does not ask players for personal information, including children under 13. Purchases and Game Center sign-in go through the device’s app store and Apple, under their family and parental controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Email dusdworks@gmail.com with privacy or support questions. DusD Works receives your email address and the information you choose to include so it can respond.
