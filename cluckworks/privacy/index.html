Cluckworks Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Cluckworks is a single-player farm-building game for iPhone, iPad and Fire tablets, made by DusD Works. It is made for kids and families, and it is built so that it does not collect any information about the people who play it.

What we collect

Nothing. Cluckworks does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no chat, and no in-app purchases.

Internet access

Cluckworks runs completely offline. The app does not ask for any permissions, and it never contacts any server.

Saved games

Your farm is saved only on your own device, inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes the saved farm.

Players can choose to copy a "farm code", a block of text that holds their farm, so they can move it to another device. The farm code holds only game data, such as buildings and coins. It stays on the device unless the player pastes it somewhere themselves.

Children

Because Cluckworks collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing for us to store, review or delete. This is in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
